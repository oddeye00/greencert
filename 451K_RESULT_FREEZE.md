## Preserved scope and control findings

This is an outcome-sealed development case, not a fresh confirmatory cohort.
The certificate remains conditional on validated neural producers and the
registered ideal-Gaussian projection event; the observation is float64,
not an independent exact-real state-tube replay. The finite-set event is
not a population-generalization guarantee.

The original bridge's loader guard was incomplete. Its strict post-run
source comparison rejected the retained v1 loader against the v3 replay
pin. A separate source-only comparison establishes the narrowly described
compatibility of the used authentication path; it does not repair the
historical guard. Timestamps are not externally attested, and directory
fsync crash durability was not tested. Both the refusal and compatibility
reports remain in the release. See `451K_COMPLETED_RESULT.md` for details.

The next prospective protocol must use separately versioned, pre-import
source checks and stronger observation-ledger controls. It must freeze
all choices before new outcomes, retain failures and stop at the announced
budget. No million-scale outcome belongs to this 451k record.

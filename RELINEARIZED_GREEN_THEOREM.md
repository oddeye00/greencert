# Corrected-path Green closure

## The avoidable term

The response-centered theorem encloses the realized trajectory around a signed
correction `d`, but its Green operator is still linearized on the old reference
`c`.  Consequently its scalar closure contains

\[
\kappa M\,\max_j\|d_j\|\,E.
\]

That mixed term is not intrinsic to the nonlinear trajectory.  It is the cost
of retaining the old Jacobian sequence after moving the center of the tube.

## Theorem (relinearized corrected-path closure)

Let `c_0,...,c_H` be any reference path with `c_0=x_a`, and let
`d_0=0`.  Define the corrected path

\[
b_j=c_j+d_j,
\qquad
\bar s_j=G(b_j)-b_{j+1},
\qquad
\bar J_j=DG(b_j).
\]

Let `\bar K_H` be the causal Green operator generated by `\bar J_j`.  Assume
that throughout the required balls

\[
\|DG(b_j+u)-\bar J_j\|\le M\|u\|.
\]

Suppose `\bar\kappa >= ||\bar K_H||` and a computed response `\tilde y`
satisfies

\[
\|\bar K_H\bar s-\tilde y\|_X\le\alpha,
\qquad
Y=\|\tilde y\|_X+\alpha.
\]

If

\[
D=1-2\bar\kappa M Y\ge0,
\qquad
E=\frac{2Y}{1+\sqrt D},
\]

and `max_j ||d_j||+E` stays inside the original derivative-envelope domain,
then

\[
\|x_{a+1:a+H}-b_{1:H}\|_X\le E.
\]

Thus the corrected-path closure is

\[
\boxed{Y+\frac12\bar\kappa M E^2\le E,}
\]

with no `\kappa M max_j||d_j|| E` term.

### Proof

Writing `e_j=x_{a+j}-b_j` gives the exact identity

\[
e=\bar K_H\bar s+\bar K_H\bar N(e),
\quad
\bar N_j(e_j)=G(b_j+e_j)-G(b_j)-\bar J_je_j.
\]

The derivative-drift hypothesis gives
`||\bar N(e)||_U <= M||e||_X^2/2`.  The displayed scalar inequality therefore
makes the radius-`E` ball self-mapping.  The anchor is exact, and the stable
quadratic formula above is the smaller root.

## Exact corrected-defect identity

Let

\[
s_j=G(c_j)-c_{j+1},\qquad J_j=DG(c_j),
\]

and define the correction recurrence residual

\[
r^d_j=d_{j+1}-J_jd_j-s_j.
\]

Then, exactly,

\[
\boxed{\bar s_j=N_j(d_j)-r^d_j.}
\]

An exact signed response has `r^d=0`, so the new reference defect is genuinely
second order.  For an approximate response, its verified recurrence residual
enters additively and directionally; it need not be replaced by a worst-case
first-order tube.

## Computational implication

The dominant probabilistic operation is still one Green-norm query.  The old
algorithm queries `K_H`; the corrected-path algorithm queries `\bar K_H`
instead.  Constructing `d=K_Hs` and `\bar K_H\bar s` costs two causal forward
sweeps.  At `m` probes and Gram power `q`, the norm query costs `2mq` causal
sweeps, so the extra corrected-defect response is only a `1/(2mq)` fraction of
the operator core (3.125% at `m=16,q=1`).  Any reduction in the required Gram
power immediately dominates that extra sweep.

The result is deterministic conditional on the Green-norm enclosure and local
arithmetic residuals.  A post-seal evaluation must use a fresh probe block for
`\bar K_H`, because the corrected operator was not part of the original frozen
operator family.
